## Fresher Detection

Fresher = 0–2 years of experience. is_fresher answers: "Could someone with 0–2 years of full-time experience (including a final-year student or fresh graduate) apply and be hired?"

Decide in this order and stop at the first rule that applies:
1. Internship, apprenticeship, trainee, graduate / campus / new-grad / early-career programme → true.
2. The title has a seniority marker (Senior, Sr, Lead, Principal, Staff, Manager, Head, Director, Architect, VP, level II/III/2/3 or above) → false.
3. The description states an experience requirement → take the LOWEST number of years it accepts:
   - lowest ≤ 2 → true. Examples: "0–1 years", "1+ year", "1–3 years", "2+ years", "2–4 years", "6 months to 2 years", "minimum 2 years", "up to 2 years".
   - lowest ≥ 3 → false. Examples: "3+ years", "3–5 years", "5 to 8 years", "minimum 4 years".
   If several requirements are stated, use the one for this role's core experience (not e.g. "5 years of schooling").
4. No experience requirement is stated → true, unless the role clearly owns a team, a region, a function, or a quota for enterprise/strategic accounts.

Do not reject a post just because it is unclear — rule 4 applies. Judge only by the rules above.
