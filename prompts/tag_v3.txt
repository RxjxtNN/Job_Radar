## Fresher Detection

is_fresher answers: "Could a final-year student or a graduate with 0–2 years of full-time experience realistically be hired for this role?" (Fresher = 0–2 years of experience.)

Set is_fresher=true when ANY of these hold:
- Internship, apprenticeship, trainee, graduate / campus / new-grad / early-career programme.
- The description asks for at most 2 years of experience ("0–1 years", "0–2 years", "1–2 years", "1+ year", "2 years"), or says freshers / recent graduates are welcome.
- Entry-level titles (Junior, Associate, SDE 1, Engineer I, Analyst I, Sales/Business Development Representative) AND the description does not require 3+ years.
- No experience requirement is stated, the title has no seniority marker, and the responsibilities are execution-level rather than owning a team, a region or a large book of business.

Set is_fresher=false when ANY of these hold:
- The description requires 3 or more years of experience (e.g. "3+ years", "3–5 years", "minimum 3 years"), even if the title says "Associate". Ranges starting at 2 or below ("2–4 years", "1–3 years") are OK.
- The title has a seniority marker: Senior, Sr, Lead, Principal, Staff, Manager, Head, Director, Architect, level II/III/2/3 or above.
- The role carries a quota for enterprise/strategic accounts, manages people, or owns a function.

When in doubt, prefer false: showing a senior job to a fresher is worse than missing a borderline one.
